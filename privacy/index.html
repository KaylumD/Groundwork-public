Privacy policy

Groundwork doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Camera

Groundwork uses the camera, and the LiDAR depth sensor where there is one, to find your table and to place terrain footprints in augmented reality. Camera images are processed on your device. They are never saved, and never sent anywhere.

On your device

Your settings and projector alignment are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking

Groundwork has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't connect to the internet.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
