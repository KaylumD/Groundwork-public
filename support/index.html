How to set up your first table, and what to do when something doesn't line up.

The marker

Print it at 100% (Actual size) on matte paper. It works on A4 or US Letter. You can also print it from the app with Print marker.

Download the marker (PDF)

Getting started

  1. Measure the printed marker from the outside edge of one corner bracket to the outside edge of the opposite one. If it isn't 180 mm, enter the real width in Settings.
  2. Lay the marker in the middle of the table with its arrow along a long edge.
  3. Hold your iPhone or iPad 40–60 cm above the marker until it locks on and shows a cyan outline.
  4. Aim the crosshair at one long edge near one end and tap Set point. Do the same near the other end of that edge.
  5. Check the lines match the table edges, tap Looks right, then take the marker off the table.
  6. Put your footprint mats down in any order. Each one turns green when the camera sees it lined up, or yellow if it's off line or the wrong piece.

Common problems

The marker won't lock on
Use even light and avoid glare on the paper. Check it printed 180 mm across; if not, enter the real width in Settings.
The board lines don't match the table
Set the two edge points again, further apart and right on the edge. Keep the marker flat and still until you tap Looks right.
Footprints stay red
The camera needs footprint mats in a colour that stands out from your play mat. Clear acrylic won't work. You can turn detection off in Settings and mark footprints placed yourself.
The projector shows the same picture as the phone
Connect with a USB-C to HDMI adapter or AirPlay to an Apple TV. If the projector can only mirror the screen, choose Full screen in projector mode.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later
  • A printer for the marker
  • For projector mode: a projector, plus a USB-C to HDMI adapter or an Apple TV
