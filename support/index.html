Support

How to set up your first table, and what to do when something doesn't line up.

Getting started

  1. Check the board size in Settings matches your play area (60″ × 44″ unless you change it): your mat, or the table if it's the board.
  2. Stand at a long edge. Hold your iPhone or iPad over the corner on your left, put the dot in the crosshair on the very corner, and tap Set corner. The card says On the table once it has found the table top.
  3. Do the same for the corner on your right.
  4. Check the lines match the board's edges, then tap Looks right.
  5. Put your footprint mats down in any order. Each one turns green when the camera sees it lined up, or yellow if it's off line or the wrong piece.

Common problems

It won't find the table
Move the phone slowly over the table for a moment so it can see the surface. Even light helps. Without LiDAR, very dark, shiny or plain tables take longer.
The board lines don't match the table
Tap Redo and set the corners again, with the dot right on each corner and the phone looking down at it. Check the board size in Settings matches your mat.
Footprints stay red
The camera needs footprint mats in a colour that stands out from your play mat. Clear acrylic won't work. You can turn detection off in Settings and mark footprints placed yourself.
The projector shows the same picture as the phone
Connect with a USB-C to HDMI adapter or AirPlay to an Apple TV. If the projector can only mirror the screen, choose Full screen in projector mode.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later
  • Works best on models with LiDAR (iPhone Pro and iPad Pro)
  • For projector mode: a projector, plus a USB-C to HDMI adapter or an Apple TV
