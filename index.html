Terrain layouts on your table

Groundwork draws every footprint, deployment zone and objective of a tournament layout on your real table, life size, using your iPhone or iPad's camera. It can also show the layout and battle effects on a projector.

Print the marker

Groundwork finds the table with a printed marker. Print it at 100% on matte paper; it should measure 180 mm across.
